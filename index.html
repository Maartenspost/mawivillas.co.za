The bush at your door. The villa all to yourself.

MAWI Villas is a small collection of three private, self-catering villas in Marloth Park — a wildlife conservancy right across the Crocodile River from Kruger National Park.

No shared pool, no dinner schedule, no crowds. Just your own villa with a private pool, quiet nights and kudu, zebra and giraffe wandering past your terrace. Kruger is a short drive away, the rhythm of the day is yours.

Why Marloth Park

Kruger on one side. Wildlife in your garden.

Marloth Park is a conservancy on the southern border of Kruger, where giraffe, zebra, kudu, warthog and wildebeest roam freely between the homes. Here, the safari often starts on your own terrace.

Instead of staying inside Kruger
Rest camps inside the park are popular, often fully booked and you share facilities. In Marloth Park you have your own villa and private pool, still with Kruger's southern gates within easy reach for early game drives.
Instead of Hazyview
Hazyview is a busy town on the road to the park. Marloth Park is quiet bush, with wildlife walking past rather than traffic, and close to the southern part of Kruger.
Instead of a hotel or lodge
No set mealtimes or shared spaces. Cook when you like, braai under the stars and live at your own pace — with a local host nearby if you need anything.
