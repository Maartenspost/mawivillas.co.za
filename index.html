    03.Marloth

    Marloth Park lies on the south bank of the Crocodile River, right across from Kruger. Inside the park there are no fences between the houses, so giraffe, zebra, kudu, wildebeest and warthog walk through the village and past your villa.

    09.FAQ

    The questions guests ask most before a stay in the bush.

    Is there malaria in Marloth Park and Kruger?

    Marloth Park and Kruger lie in a malaria area. Please check with a travel clinic or your doctor well before you travel about the right protection for your dates. Insect repellent and long sleeves in the evening help too.

    When are the Kruger gates open?

    Entrance gates open between 05:30 and 06:00 and close between 17:30 and 18:30, depending on the month. See the table in the Kruger section. Leave time to get out before closing: late arrivals are refused entry.

    Verify yearly

    How does check-in work?

    Check-in is self check-in. You collect your keys and codes at Field Security in the Marloth Park Bush Centre and drive on to your villa. We send you all details before arrival.

    Field Security opening hours

    What happens during power cuts?

    Every villa runs on solar panels with battery backup, so you keep power when the grid goes down.

    What should I bring?

    Binoculars, a camera with a zoom lens, sun protection and a hat, insect repellent, a torch for the evening and light long sleeves. Linen, towels and a fully equipped kitchen are ready in the villa.

    Check the list

    Is it safe with the wild animals around?

    Yes, if you respect them. Never feed the animals, keep your distance, drive slowly and give animals right of way. Be extra careful at dusk and at night.

    How far are the gates and the shops?

    Crocodile Bridge gate is about 14 km away and Malelane gate about 35 km. Marloth Park has a few shops and restaurants; for bigger supermarkets you drive to Komatipoort or Malelane.
