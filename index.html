    03.Marloth

    Marloth Park lies on the south bank of the Crocodile River, right across from Kruger. Inside the park there are no fences between the houses, so giraffe, zebra, kudu, wildebeest and warthog walk through the village and past your villa.

    life in a bush village

    Marloth Park is a wildlife sanctuary where people and animals share the same space. Giraffe, zebra, kudu, wildebeest and warthog wander between the houses, and from the riverbank you look straight across into Kruger. Inside the park lies Lionspruit, a fenced game reserve you can drive through.

    The park has a few restaurants, a small shopping centre and a fuel station, and the larger supermarkets in Komatipoort and Malelane are a short drive away. Evenings are quiet: no streetlights, just the sounds of the bush and a sky full of stars.

    highlights of the south

    Southern Kruger is the most wildlife-rich part of the park. These are the places we send our guests first.

    Lower Sabie

    A rest camp on the Sabie River, about 34 km from Crocodile Bridge gate. Have breakfast on the deck while elephants come down to drink.

    Sunset Dam

    Just outside Lower Sabie: hippos, crocodiles and water birds, at their best in the late afternoon light.

    Crocodile Bridge

    The gate closest to Marloth Park, with open plains to the north that are known for buffalo, rhino and lion.

    Berg-en-Dal

    Hilly, rocky bush near Malelane gate, with a walking trail along the camp fence and a good chance of leopard.

    our story

    We are Willemijn and Maarten, and in 2027 we are swapping the Netherlands for South Africa with our two young daughters. Mawi Villas is the start of that new chapter: three houses on the edge of Kruger, run the way we would like to be welcomed ourselves.

    We are a family that loves to be outdoors. Our holidays are road trips with a rooftop tent on the car, the girls in the back and no fixed plan, and South Africa is the country we keep coming back to.

    why south africa

    South Africa got under our skin long before we decided to move. Maarten’s work brought him here again and again over the years, and for a long time he volunteered as a teacher for young people from the townships. Every trip ended with the same question on the flight home: why are we leaving?

    It is the space and the light, the warmth of the people and the feeling that any day can still surprise you. It is a country where children grow up outdoors, where dinner is a braai under the stars and where the landscapes change from mountains and vineyards to bush and ocean within a day’s drive.

    why kruger

    And then there is the bush. Nothing compares to the first hour after the gates open: mist hanging over the river, a herd of elephants crossing the road in front of you, the call of a fish eagle somewhere out of sight. In those moments the car goes quiet, even with two little girls on the back seat.

    We love the rhythm of a safari day: up before dawn, a long lazy afternoon by the pool, back out for the golden hour and a fire at night under a sky so full of stars you hardly recognise it. Marloth Park gives you that rhythm in a house of your own, with giraffe at the breakfast table and Kruger just across the river. That is the feeling we want to share.

    how we host

    Willemijn spent many years leading large projects in marketing and advertising and has renovated and furnished several houses in the Netherlands. She brings that eye for detail to every villa and is your host from your first message until the moment you drive away.

    We know from our own travels what makes a stay work: a good bed, a kitchen you can really cook in, honest tips instead of sales talk, and someone who replies quickly. That is the promise behind every Mawi villa.

    10.FAQ

    The questions guests ask most before a stay in the bush.

    Is there malaria in Marloth Park and Kruger?

    Marloth Park and Kruger lie in a malaria area. Please check with a travel clinic or your doctor well before you travel about the right protection for your dates. Insect repellent and long sleeves in the evening help too.

    When are the Kruger gates open?

    Entrance gates open between 05:30 and 06:00 and close between 17:30 and 18:30, depending on the month. See the table in the Kruger section. Leave time to get out before closing: late arrivals are refused entry.

    Verify yearly

    How does check-in work?

    Check-in is self check-in. You collect your keys and codes at Field Security in the Marloth Park Bush Centre and drive on to your villa. We send you all details before arrival.

    Field Security opening hours

    What happens during power cuts?

    Every villa runs on solar panels with battery backup, so you keep power when the grid goes down.

    What should I bring?

    Binoculars, a camera with a zoom lens, sun protection and a hat, insect repellent, a torch for the evening and light long sleeves. Linen, towels and a fully equipped kitchen are ready in the villa.

    Check the list

    Is it safe with the wild animals around?

    Yes, if you respect them. Never feed the animals, keep your distance, drive slowly and give animals right of way. Be extra careful at dusk and at night.

    How far are the gates and the shops?

    Crocodile Bridge gate is about 14 km away and Malelane gate about 35 km. Marloth Park has a few shops and restaurants; for bigger supermarkets you drive to Komatipoort or Malelane.

    Verify driving distances Shop distances

    Can you arrange a guided game drive?

    Yes. We work with experienced local field guides for sunrise, sunset and full-day drives in Kruger, with pick-up at your villa. Send us your dates and we book it for you.

    Can we have breakfast for an early game drive?

    Yes. Order a safari breakfast basket and we deliver it to your door before the gates open, with coffee in flasks and food for the road.

    Do we need a 4x4?

    No. The main roads in southern Kruger are tarred and the gravel roads are fine for a normal car. Many roads in Marloth Park are sand, which a regular car handles well if you drive slowly.

    Can we cycle in Marloth Park?

    Yes, cycling is a lovely way to explore. Keep your distance from animals, stay on the roads and avoid cycling at dusk. We can arrange bicycles for your stay.

    Are the villas suitable for children?

    Yes. Mawi Two and Mawi Three are made for families. Children should always be supervised around the pool and the wildlife.
